# Sources, scope, and review

## What this library is

This repository is a personal knowledge and process-design library. Its diagrams are useful prompts for discovery, requirements workshops, and portfolio discussion. They are not approved operating procedures or evidence of how a specific organisation currently works.

Some legacy examples mention banks, payment platforms, credit-reporting services, and regulatory concepts. Those references are contextual only. Names and trademarks belong to their respective owners, and their appearance does not imply affiliation, approval, or endorsement.

## What to verify before reuse

Before using a flow for a real product, policy, or control, validate at least:

- the current process owner, system of record, and approved channels;
- jurisdiction-specific legal, regulatory, and data-handling requirements;
- delegated authority, risk thresholds, segregation-of-duties controls, and exception handling;
- payment-scheme rules, product capabilities, and service-level assumptions; and
- customer communications and accessibility requirements.

## Citation policy

Use primary sources wherever a diagram or guide makes a factual claim about a regulator, scheme, bank, product, platform, or policy. Add a named **Sources** section with stable links and an access/review date. Do not use unlinked numeric citation markers.

Existing legacy notes that contain bare markers such as `[1]` are not a verified bibliography. Treat them as draft commentary until the underlying source is added or the claim is rewritten as a generic example.
