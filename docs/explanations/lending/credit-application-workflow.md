# Credit-application workflow — companion guide

> **Illustrative example.** This guide adds reading context for a conceptual process. It is not an official bank policy, product specification, operating procedure, or compliance advice.

[View the diagram](../../../diagrams/lending/credit-application-workflow.mmd) · [Browse the catalog](../../diagram-catalog.md)

## Purpose

The diagram models a staged credit-application workflow from portfolio creation through prescreening, data collection, analyst review, multi-tier approval, and conditional offer issuance.

## Reading the flow

1. Prescreening either allows the application to continue or sends it to a defined manual-review or closure outcome.
2. Data entry is iterative: an analyst can request missing or inconsistent information before assessment continues.
3. Decisions are represented at each approval tier so rejection, request-for-information, and escalation outcomes remain visible.
4. The diagram ends at conditional offer issuance; it deliberately does not model acceptance, fulfilment, disbursement, or servicing.

## Review prompts

- What rules may be automated, and which require human decisioning or documented overrides?
- What are the approval authorities, delegations, and escalation triggers for each tier?
- How are rejection and manual-review outcomes communicated and retained?
- Which downstream flow begins after a conditional offer is issued?
