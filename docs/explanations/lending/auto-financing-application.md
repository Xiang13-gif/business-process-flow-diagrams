# Auto-financing application — companion guide

> **Illustrative example.** This guide adds reading context for a conceptual process. It is not an official bank policy, product specification, operating procedure, or compliance advice.

[View the diagram](../../../diagrams/lending/auto-financing-application.mmd) · [Browse the catalog](../../diagram-catalog.md)

## Purpose

The diagram shows a high-level vehicle-financing journey: application capture, documentation, credit assessment, vehicle validation, underwriting, offer acceptance, disbursement, and servicing setup.

## Reading the flow

1. The journey starts with customer and vehicle information plus an initial identity/risk screen.
2. Incomplete or invalid documents return to the collection stage.
3. Credit and verification outcomes determine whether an application proceeds to underwriting or exits.
4. Vehicle valuation and affordability are separate decision gates before an offer is issued.
5. Accepted offers progress to signing, disbursement, registration, and account activation.

## Review prompts

- Which documents, identity checks, and vehicle fields are required for the actual product?
- What is the approved exception path for fraud, AML, affordability, valuation, or incomplete-document outcomes?
- Which system owns each decision, and where is the auditable decision record held?
- What terms must be disclosed before offer acceptance and disbursement?
